# AWS Support Plans - Paid vs. Free

AWS offers tiered support plans: Basic (free, includes account/billing support and access to documentation/forums), Developer (paid, business-hours email access to technical support), Business (24/7 support with faster response times, suited for production workloads), and Enterprise (fastest response times, a dedicated Technical Account Manager, for mission-critical systems). Higher tiers cost more but provide faster guaranteed response times and more proactive guidance.
