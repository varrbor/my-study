# Sign Up for an AWS Account and Use the AWS Free Tier

Creating an AWS account requires an email, a credit card (for billing verification), and phone verification. Once registered, you get access to the AWS Free Tier, which offers limited free usage of many services for 12 months (e.g., 750 hours/month of a small EC2 instance, 5GB of S3 storage) plus some services that are always free up to a limit. The Free Tier is ideal for learning and experimenting without incurring charges, but it's important to monitor usage since exceeding limits results in billing.
