# Elasticity - Scaling Your Architecture to Meet Demand and Reduce Costs

Elasticity is the ability of a cloud architecture to automatically grow or shrink resources in response to real-time demand, rather than being sized for peak load at all times. This means you can add capacity during traffic spikes and remove it during quiet periods, paying only for what's actually needed. Elasticity is a core cloud computing advantage over traditional, fixed-capacity data centers, reducing both waste and the risk of being overwhelmed by unexpected load.
