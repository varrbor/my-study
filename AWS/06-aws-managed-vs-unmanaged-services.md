# Understand AWS Managed vs. Unmanaged Services

Unmanaged services (like a raw EC2 instance) give you full control over the OS and software but require you to handle patching, scaling, backups, and maintenance yourself. Managed services (like RDS or DynamoDB) have AWS handle the underlying infrastructure, patching, and often scaling/backups automatically, letting you focus on your application instead of operations. The tradeoff is flexibility and control versus convenience and reduced operational burden—managed services are usually easier to run but offer less low-level customization.
